LOCAL LEDGER - START HERE
=========================

What it is
----------
A budgeting ledger that runs on your own computer. It never connects to your bank,
and your numbers never leave this computer.


How to start
------------
1. Unzip the whole folder somewhere permanent, like Documents. Don't run it from
   inside the zip file.
2. Double-click "Start Personal Finance Ledger". Your web browser opens the ledger.
   (If Windows says "Windows protected your PC", click "More info", then "Run anyway".
   That message appears for any program that isn't from a big publisher. This one only
   runs on your own computer.)
3. The first time, pick how you budget:
      - Every two weeks: each paycheck is its own budget, or
      - Monthly: one budget per month.
   You can change this later on the Plan tab. If you'd like to look around first,
   click "Load sample data first" to see it with made-up numbers.
4. On the Plan tab, add your income, your regular bills and your budgets.
5. Bring in your bank transactions (next section).


Bringing in your transactions  (this part is manual)
------------------------------------------------------
The ledger does NOT connect to your bank. You bring the transactions in yourself:

  1. Log in to your bank's website and download your transactions as a CSV file
     (or an .xlsx Excel file). Pick a date range, like the last 90 days.
  2. In the ledger, click the Import button (the arrow pointing down) on the left
     edge and choose your file. The ledger works out which column is the date, the
     amount and the description on its own, even when your bank names them oddly or
     splits money out and money in into two columns.
     The FIRST time (and any time it isn't sure) it shows you what it found. Check
     it, and check the line "money spent shows as": some banks print spending as
     positive numbers and others as negative. If your spending ever shows up as
     income afterwards, that setting is the one to flip. Then click Import file.
  3. Do it again whenever you want things up to date. Once a week is plenty.
     Importing the same dates twice is safe: repeats are skipped. After the first
     time, files it recognizes import in one click.

New transactions are sorted into categories using "rules" (Plan tab > Rules). The first
time, you'll sort some by hand; after that most are filed automatically.


Your data
---------
Everything you enter is saved in the "My Ledger Data" folder next to this file.
To back it up, copy that folder. Before every change, the ledger saves the old version
in My Ledger Data\backups, and the Undo button at the top puts it back.


Stopping and starting
---------------------
Closing the browser tab leaves the ledger running quietly in the background. Double-click
"Stop Personal Finance Ledger" to shut it down. "Start Personal Finance Ledger" starts it again any time.


Updating to a newer version
---------------------------
Download the new zip and unzip it somewhere else. Copy ITS "app" and "python" folders
over the ones you have now. Leave "My Ledger Data" alone and your numbers carry over.


Good to know
------------
- It only opens on this computer. Nobody else on your network can see it.
- It's a budgeting tool, not financial advice.
- If something looks wrong, nothing is lost: use Undo, or restore a file from
  My Ledger Data\backups.
